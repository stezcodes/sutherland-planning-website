About Us

Town & Country Planning Consultants.

Sutherland Planning Ltd was founded in July 2026 by Jim Bailey and Chris Lecointe, two town planning professionals, each with over 40 years' experience and with a shared commitment to high-quality, honest and accessible planning advice.

Director

Jim Bailey

Jim started his career as a Planning Advice Assistant at Rushmoor Council in 1986, before moving into the private sector in 1989. He went on to hold senior roles at Bryant Homes and his own consultancy, then joined DLP in 1996 as a Senior Director running their Berkshire office.

He later worked at Alliance Planning and Pegasus Planning, before Chris recruited him as a Planning Director at RPS (later Tetratech) in Abingdon. After a career break travelling in 2024, he joined Sutherland Property and Legal Services in Plymouth, and in July 2026 he set up Sutherland Planning Ltd with Chris.

jim@sutherlandplanning.com
Director

Chris Lecointe

Chris began his career with Southampton City Council in 1985, in the development control department. After a few years he decided to join the newly emerging private planning consultancy sector, in particular Chapman Warren in Wroughton near Swindon.

He spent 3 years there and then moved to Oldfield King as a Director based in Bristol, then after another few years joined RPS in Oxfordshire. He was Operational Director at RPS (now Tetratech) for 17 years, looking after the Oxford, Cambridge, Edinburgh and Glasgow offices. He managed a multidisciplinary team and for the last ten years specialised in renewable and low carbon energy projects.

chris@sutherlandplanning.com
