Home Services

Planning services for homes, farms & businesses

Whatever stage your project is at, we can help — from an informal first opinion through to a full appeal. Get in touch and we'll tell you honestly where you stand.

Planning Applications

Preparation, management and submission of planning applications for:

Residential Development

  • From single dwellings to small to medium-sized residential schemes
  • Rural workers' dwellings
  • Self-build dwellings
  • Agricultural buildings (including Class Q conversions) and diversification projects

Energy Development

  • Battery storage
  • Solar farms
  • Energy from waste

Commercial Development

  • Commercial and mixed use proposals
  • Tourism, leisure and outdoor recreation development

We handle the full process — from initial appraisal to securing a final decision.

Pre-Application Advice & Strategy

Early-stage guidance to help you understand constraints, opportunities and likely outcomes. We provide:

  • Site appraisals
  • Identification of constraints
  • National and local policy analysis
  • Risk assessment
  • Recommended next steps

A robust strategy at the outset saves time, cost and uncertainty later.

Planning Statements & Supporting Documents

Clear, robust and policy-driven planning statements, covering:

  • Planning history
  • Policy justification
  • Site suitability
  • Detailed analysis
  • Planning balance

Our statements are concise, persuasive and tailored to local planning authority expectations.

Enforcement & Appeals

Support with:

  • Enforcement notices and negotiations
  • Certificates of Lawfulness
  • Written representations
  • Informal Hearings
  • Expert witness at Public Inquiries

We help you challenge decisions or resolve issues constructively.

Project Management

Specialist expertise in:

  • Providing the planning lead and managing multidisciplinary project teams
  • Managing Environmental Impact Assessment and production of Environmental Statements
  • Providing planning lead or support for Nationally Significant Infrastructure Projects under the Planning Act 2008

We understand the realities of business and the policies that shape it. We can help landowners to maximise the development potential of their land holding.

Local Plan Representations

Professional submissions to emerging local and neighbourhood plans, ensuring your land and development interests are promoted effectively and strategically.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us about your project and we'll point you in the right direction — free of charge.
